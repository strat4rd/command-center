Friday pickups
On Friday, the run is mostly easy.
  1. 10 minutes easy jog
  2. Then 4–6 rounds of: 20 seconds quicker, then 90 seconds very easy jog or walk
  3. Finish the rest of the run easy
The pickups should feel smooth and light, not like sprint intervals.
How to handle Thursday
Thursday is intentionally flexible. Use this rule:
How you feelWhat to do
Legs feel normalEasy run
Legs feel heavy from Wednesday strengthBrisk walk
Low energy / poor sleepWalk
Feeling good but not motivated to runWalk is acceptable
Thursday is not the key run. The most important runs are Saturday, then Tuesday, then Friday.
